## Correctness

Final publication requires:

- 10,000 matched locations per engine and tier;
- zero unexplained mismatches over 1 metre;
- exhaustive validation of at least 100 deterministic locations; and
- a closest point on the selected road for every row.

The machine-readable result is `evidence/correctness-summary.json`.

## Interpretation checklist

The final narrative must distinguish:

- preparation, cold, warm-up, and measured query time;
- physical-core scaling from the SMT endpoint;
- Sedona CPU work from Kinetica's configured GPU work;
- effects of road-network density;
- performance conclusions from correctness conclusions; and
- observed results from hypotheses.

No vendor-general conclusion should be inferred from this single host.
